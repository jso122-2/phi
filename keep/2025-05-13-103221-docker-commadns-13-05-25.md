-v …:/models/ft-batch-final mounts your folder into the container.

-p 8080:80 exposes port 80 in the container as localhost:8080.

The container will load your model (including your safetensors, tokenizer, config, etc.) and serve it under a simple HTTP API.

4) Talk to your model via curl
Once Docker says “ready,” in a separate shell run:

bash
Copy
Edit
curl -X POST http://localhost:8080/v1/models/ft-batch-final:predict \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{"inputs":"Explain the principle of Occam’s razor:"}'
You’ll get back JSON with your model’s generated text.
