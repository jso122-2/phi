### **1. Competitor Analysis**
- **Market Share Comparison**: Compare eBay with competitors like Amazon, Etsy, or Walmart in terms of sales, market trends, and user base.
- **SWOT Analysis**: Identify eBay's strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats.

---

### **2. Customer Demographics and Segmentation**
- **Key Buyer Groups**: Analyze the demographics (age, gender, location) of eBay's buyers and sellers.
- **Behavioral Segmentation**: Study patterns like purchase frequency, average spend, and categories they shop in (electronics, collectibles, etc.).
- **Geographic Insights**: Identify high-performing regions and untapped markets.

---

### **3. Pricing and Value Perception**
- **Price Elasticity**: Investigate how price changes impact demand for key product categories.
- **Competitive Pricing**: Compare prices for popular items on eBay versus competitors.
- **Value Proposition**: Assess customer perception of eBay's pricing fairness and platform fees.

---

### **4. Marketing Channel Performance**
- **Paid Advertising**: Evaluate the effectiveness of PPC campaigns, such as Google Ads or eBay's promoted listings.
- **Social Media Presence**: Analyze eBay’s engagement and audience growth across social platforms.
- **Email Campaigns**: Review email marketing metrics like open rates and click-through rates.

---

### **5. Seller Performance Analysis**
- **Seller Segmentation**: Divide sellers into categories (small businesses, power sellers, casual sellers) and assess their contributions.
- **Seller Churn**: Investigate why sellers leave the platform and how to improve retention.
- **Top Categories**: Identify which product categories drive the most revenue and engagement.

- **Loyalty Program Analysis**: Evaluate the impact of eBay’s programs like eBay Bucks or subscription services (e.g., eBay Plus).
- **Repeat Purchases**: Identify factors driving customer loyalty and repeat purchases.
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Estimate how much revenue each customer generates over their lifetime.
