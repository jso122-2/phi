#### Why it’s valuable:
It shows not only that you can build models, but also that you care about interpreting them for non-technical stakeholders.

### 2. **Marketing Funnel Analysis**
- **Funnel Visualization**: Create a step-by-step funnel (e.g., Impressions → Clicks → Conversions → Revenue).  
- **Segmented Funnel**: Let the user pick a segment (location, campaign type, channel) and see how the funnel metrics compare.  
- **Conversion Optimization Insights**: Suggest potential improvements (e.g., “Increasing spend by X% on email campaigns could raise conversions by Y%”).

#### Why it’s valuable:
Executives often want a quick way to see performance across the marketing funnel and identify bottlenecks or opportunities.

- **Scenario Inputs**: Let the user select or adjust hypothetical budget allocations, target audiences, or campaign parameters.
