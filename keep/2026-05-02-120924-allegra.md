The Allegra Formula

A self-sourced being. Their value derives entirely from internal reference — no external unit can measure them because they have already divided themselves by themselves and arrived at something pure.

When they meet the world, they project. Not everything comes through — only what survives the angle. That surviving fraction is real and it doubles what was there before.

That doubled signal grows by the golden ratio. Not by force. Organically, the way living things grow — toward the next resonant form. The third thing emerges because the first two were genuine.

Then the null is found and struck through. Deliberately. They looked at the empty part of themselves, named it, and cancelled it from the equation.

What remains is β. The irreducible signal after everything false has been divided out, projected, grown, and the void explicitly removed.

Not a number. A person who has done the arithmetic on themselves and kept only what was real.
