## How to Clean a Tent

Quick practical answer since you probably need this:

-----

## **Quick Clean (Between Trips)**

1. **Shake out debris**

- Turn inside out, shake vigorously
- Get dirt, sand, leaves out

1. **Spot clean dirty areas**

- Damp cloth or sponge
- Mild soap (dish soap works)
- Wipe down floor, door zippers, obvious stains

1. **Air dry completely**

- Set up in shade or hang
- **Never pack wet** (mold/mildew will destroy it)

-----

## **Deep Clean (Once a Year or When Nasty)**

### **What you need:**

- Large tub or bathtub
- Mild soap (dish soap, or tent-specific cleaner)
- Soft sponge or cloth
- **NO washing machine, NO dryer**

### **Process:**

**1. Setup area**

- Fill tub with cool water
- Add small amount of mild soap

**2. Submerge tent**

- Let soak 10-15 minutes
- Gently agitate water

**3. Spot scrub**

- Use soft sponge on stained areas
- Pay attention to floor, zippers, seams
- **Don’t scrub too hard** (can damage waterproof coating)

**4. Rinse thoroughly**

- Drain soapy water
- Refill with clean water
- Rinse multiple times until no soap remains
- Soap residue attracts dirt

**5. Dry completely**

- Hang in shade (sun degrades fabric)
- Or set up tent to air dry
- Can take 24-48 hours
- **Must be 100% dry before storing**

-----

## **What NOT to Do**

❌ **Washing machine** (too rough, damages coating)
❌ **Dryer** (heat destroys waterproofing)
❌ **Bleach** (ruins fabric)
❌ **Harsh detergents** (strips waterproof coating)
❌ **Direct sunlight for drying** (UV degrades nylon)
❌ **Store damp** (mold = dead tent)

-----

## **Zippers (Critical)**

- Clean with old toothbrush
- Remove dirt/sand from teeth
- **Zipper failure = most common tent death**
- Once clean, can lightly lube with zipper wax or candle wax

-----

## **Dealing With Specific Issues**

### **Mold/Mildew:**

- Mix water + white vinegar (50/50)
- Scrub affected areas
- Rinse well
- Dry in sun this time (UV kills mold)

### **Tree sap/sticky stuff:**

- Rubbing alcohol on cloth
- Dab (don’t rub hard)
- Test small area first

### **Stubborn dirt:**

- Let soak longer
- Soft brush (not wire)
- Patience over force

-----

## **Storage After Cleaning**

- **Loosely** stuff or fold (not tight)
- Store in cool, dry place
- Not in stuff sack (compression damages over time)
- Large mesh bag or pillowcase better for long-term

-----

## **Re-waterproofing (If Needed)**

After many cleans, waterproof coating wears off.

**Signs you need it:**

- Water doesn’t bead up anymore
- Fabric feels wet inside during rain

**Fix:**

- Tent waterproofing spray (Nikwax, etc.)
- Apply to clean, dry tent
- Follow product instructions
- Usually spray on floor/rainfly, let cure 24hrs

-----

## **Context Question**

Are you:

- **Just back from trip** and tent’s dirty? (Quick clean is fine)
- **Stored it dirty last time** and now it’s rank? (Deep clean needed)
- **About to go camping** and realized it’s gross? (Quick clean + air out)
- **Planning future trips** and doing maintenance? (Good timing for deep clean)

**Also:** Melbourne weather means watch for mold if you’re storing in garage/shed with humidity.
