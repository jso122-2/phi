What do you do when you have to train a machine learning pipeline but only have 13 peices of data 

During a recent contract I designed and built a machine learning pipeline to format and parse documents into internal templates as a one click execution.

Sounds simple enough but I was initially only provided with 13 peices of data, many in my position would say well I need more data or it’s simply not possible.

Imagine In this context data is like funding, for a creative agency it could be considered similar to a budget the more data the more availability of resources.  

You see for a machine learning model to function at what it was trained on it needs to be trained.
Anything it does not receive during its training stage it does not know now, but more than that it believes nothing else exists. Anything not fed into the training sequence does not exist to a machine learning pipeline. Particularly one working locally as an internal tool.

As a result 13 pieces just doesn’t cut it, instead of making this my clients problem and saying I can’t work with such little data, I made it my problem.
I took every morsel of flavour and style out of those 13 examples and generated out 500 perfect copies not literal copies as mirrored text. But sooner they were style copies indisfusishable for a machine learning pipeline to tell apart. So now I had 513 peices of data and then with mutations that very quickly became 5000

With some effective custom FIASS matrices and smart k kerneling this sentence encoder was bulletproof. It would perform document formatting in ———- insert metrics ———
In under 200ms of latency.

The tool was made now it just had to be deployed.
As a sole contractor it’s never you that can’t deliver, if you get no data or no equivalent resources that is and never was your problem
