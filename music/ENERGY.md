# ENERGY

#hub

Hub shard 1 — tempo, loudness, and valence energy blend.

Receives injections from three Spotify audio features:
- `energy` (direct, [0, 1])
- `tempo` (normalised: BPM / 200)
- `loudness` (shifted from [-60, 0] dB, then / 60)

The blended signal is the mean of all contributing features that arrive.
