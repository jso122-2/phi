# MOOD

#hub

Hub shard 2 — emotional arc, valence, and danceability.

Receives injections from:
- `valence` ([0, 1] — musical positiveness)
- `danceability` ([0, 1] — rhythm regularity, beat strength, tempo stability)

The blended signal (mean of valence + danceability) represents the emotional
and physical engagement level of the current track.

## Spoke context

Notes linked here describe emotional landscapes: melancholy, euphoria,
tension, release, contemplative drift, dancefloor pressure.
