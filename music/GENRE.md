# GENRE

#hub

Hub shard 3 — style classification, speechiness, acousticness.

Receives injections from:
- `speechiness` ([0, 1] — ratio of spoken word in the track)
- `acousticness` ([0, 1] — confidence that the track is acoustic)
- `instrumentalness` ([0, 1] — predicts absence of vocals)

The blended signal encodes where the track sits on the
acoustic/electronic and vocal/instrumental axes.
